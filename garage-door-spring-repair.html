Safe & Reliable Spring Replacement Services

A broken garage door spring is not only an inconvenience; it is a significant safety hazard. At Arrowhead Door Co, we specialize in diagnosing and replacing both torsion and extension springs for homes and businesses across Independence, Missouri. Our technicians arrive with fully stocked trucks, ready to install high-cycle springs that are rated for tens of thousands of cycles, ensuring long-lasting performance.

Signs Your Garage Door Spring Needs Repair:

  • Loud Bang: A sound resembling a gunshot indicates a snapped torsion spring.
  • Door Won't Open: The opener motor runs, but the door barely moves or reverses immediately.
  • Visible Gap: A noticeable separation in the torsion spring coil when the door is closed.
  • Crooked Door: The door appears uneven when closing due to a broken extension spring on one side.

Warning: Never attempt to repair or replace a garage door spring yourself. The tension is extremely dangerous and requires professional-grade tools and training.

While you're addressing spring issues, it's also a great time to evaluate your garage door opener to ensure the entire system operates smoothly and safely.
