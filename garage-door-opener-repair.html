Restore Convenience and Security to Your Home

A malfunctioning garage door opener can disrupt your daily routine and compromise your home's security. Arrowhead Door Co provides comprehensive diagnostic, repair, and replacement services for all major garage door opener brands, including LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, and Craftsman. Whether your motor is humming but not moving, the gears are stripped, or the smart-home connectivity has failed, our technicians can resolve the issue efficiently.

Our Opener Services Include:

  • Motor & Gear Replacement: Fixing stripped nylon or metal gears that cause grinding noises.
  • Sensor Alignment: Resolving safety eye issues that prevent the door from closing.
  • Smart Opener Upgrades: Installing Wi-Fi-enabled openers compatible with your smartphone.
  • Belt, Chain, and Screw Drive Repairs: Tailored solutions based on your noise tolerance and usage needs.
