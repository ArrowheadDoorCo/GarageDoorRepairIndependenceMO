Premium Components for Long-Lasting Repairs

The longevity of your garage door system depends entirely on the quality of its components. Arrowhead Door Co supplies and installs only high-grade, durable parts for all garage door repair needs in Independence, Missouri. We avoid cheap, generic alternatives, opting instead for high-cycle torsion springs, nylon-coated steel rollers, and heavy-duty lift cables that ensure smooth, quiet, and safe operation for years to come.

🔗 Additional Resource: Find the best components and learn about our part warranties on our dedicated page for Parts for Independence Garage Door Repair.

Common Parts We Replace:

  • Rollers: Upgrading from noisy metal rollers to quiet, smooth nylon-coated steel rollers.
  • Cables: Replacing frayed or snapped lift cables to prevent door imbalance.
  • Hinges: Swapping out bent or worn center and top hinges to keep door sections aligned.
  • Weatherstripping: Installing new bottom seals to keep out Missouri rain, pests, and drafts.

Need a full system check? Pair your parts replacement with our comprehensive spring and cable inspection to ensure total system safety.
